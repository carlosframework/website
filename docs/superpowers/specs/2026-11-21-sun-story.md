## What I took from the deck

The deck is Paul's HalfStack Vienna talk (`halfstack-vienna-build`; the dot
is in `engine/deck.js`, `engine/deck.css` and `acts/circles.py`, and its
rules are in that repo's design spec under “the whole journey”). What made
the dot work there:

1. **It is almost nothing.** A point, a halo, a thin ring and a faint glow,
   all one colour. The spec there says “It isn't a figure and isn't an
   egg.” Because it is so plain it can be on every slide without wearing
   out.
2. **Its light is its voice.** Three states carry the whole talk: lit,
   turned down, out. It never changes shape. The room read “the light
   goes out” without being told.
3. **It is the only amber thing.** One accent, used for one meaning. Every
   other line in the deck is pale grey.
4. **Circles within circles.** Its own ring is what is its own. Things
   inside the ring are local; big circles outside are rented. Other people
   are the same mark in a pale tone, each with their own ring.
5. **The world changes round it.** The dot stays small and steady while
   the scene moves. It is never the biggest thing on the slide.
6. **It glides.** One easing for every move (0.6 s), a slow fade when it
   goes out (1.4 s), and nothing else. With reduced motion each state is
   simply there.
7. **Flat drawing.** The glow is five faint flat discs, not a gradient or
   a blur, so it is crisp at any size and costs nothing to draw.

What does not carry over: the deck is always charcoal, and the homepage is
light by default. Amber on charcoal glows; amber on pale paper needs a thin
darker line to hold its edge. Frame `h4` shows the hero in the dark theme,
where it looks most like the deck.

## The character

Frame `s` is the character sheet.

**What it is.** A CARLOS app, seen from the inside, drawn as a small sun:
an amber circle with a soft glow, a thin ring round it and a smaller
circle at its centre. No face, no eyes, no mouth, no rays. It speaks with
its light and its size.

**The cell idea stays, as a figure of speech.** It still swells and
shrinks, divides into one each, dims to sleep and brightens on a request.
The page no longer says “cell” anywhere (round 3 said it once), and never
says nucleus, membrane or any other biology word.

### Anatomy

Four circles, all on one centre. `r` is the radius of the ring.

| Part | Size | Drawn as | Stands for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glow | 1.08, 1.28, 1.5 and 1.75 r | Four flat amber discs at 7.5% each, stacked | It is awake |
| Ring | r | A 1.5 px line, 60% | Its boundary: what is its own |
| Body | 0.6 r | Solid amber, 1.5 px darker edge on a light page | The app |
| Core | 0.23 r | A paler circle | Nothing in particular. It is never a database, and never labelled |

Colours: amber `#ffb454` (the deck's accent), bright `#ffc66e`, squished
`#f3c891`, core `#fff0d2`, edge and ring `#c97a16`. At night and in the
dark theme the edge and ring are the amber itself and each glow disc is
5%.

One meaning each, and no borrowing: **the glow means awake, the ring means
its own boundary, a dashed line between two suns means glue.** Nothing
else on the page is amber.

### Sizes

The size is the ring's diameter. The glow needs 0.75 r of clear space
beyond it.

| Where | Size |
|---|---|
| Hero | 120 px; 96 px at 390 |
| A chapter's main sun | 160 to 240 px |
| The quiz, beside the pile | 68 px; 56 px at 390 |
| Inside a picture | 32 px and up |
| The rail's six dots | 13 px: body and ring only |

Under 20 px the core and the glow are left out (`is-small`).

### States

| State | What changes | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| **Rest** | Nothing. A live one breathes: the body and ring grow 3.5% and the glow fades to 72%. | 4 s, ease in and out, the only loop |
| **Swell** | Body and ring to 118%, glow to 112%. | 400 ms out, `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.3,1)`; 600 ms back |
| **Shrink** | Everything to 80%. | 400 ms |
| **Squish** | Body, core and ring scale to 120% wide and 72% tall from the foot, so it flattens against what it stands on. The body pales and the glow drops to 35%. | 400 ms, ease out; back in 500 ms |
| **Dividing** | A second body slides out of the first, on a slant. The one ring stretches round both and fades. | 500 ms, the deck's glide, `cubic-bezier(.45,0,.25,1)` |
| **Divided** | Two suns. Each draws its own ring. | 300 ms a ring |
| **Asleep** | Glow off, core off, body to 34%, ring to 32%, everything to 86%. | 1.4 s down, as the deck's light goes out; 300 ms up |
| **Set** | Asleep, and half under a horizon line. A scene device, for chapters with a ground. | sinks with the 1.4 s |
| **Bright** | Body to 108% and a hotter amber, core white, glow 1.7 times stronger. One thin ring goes out from the boundary to 130% and stays there. | 300 ms, then the ring in 600 ms |
| **Ghost** | A dashed ring and nothing else: where it was, or where it could be. | a still |
| **Walled** | The ring at 3 px and full strength. A modifier, for the chapter about the wall. | 300 ms |

Waking is 300 ms on purpose. The page says an app wakes in under half a
second, and the character should not take longer than the claim.

**Reduced motion.** No transitions and no breathing. Each state is a
still, and the bright state's extra ring is simply there.

### Rules

1. **Circles only.** No outline path, no wobble, no blob. A squish is a
   circle scaled to an ellipse, and that is the furthest it bends.
2. **Nothing in a level pair.** Two suns side by side by themselves read
   as eyes. So it divides on a slant, and a divided pair is drawn on a
   slant or as three or more. Found while drawing: the first draft of the
   “divided” pose looked like an owl.
3. **The core stays centred.** Off centre it becomes an eye looking
   sideways.
4. **Nothing is drawn inside it.** It is opaque. What it holds is shown as
   small points on a faint orbit inside its ring (chapter 1), or said in
   words.
5. **Copies gather. They never join.** Two suns becoming one would be two
   customers sharing data. Unchanged from round 3.
6. **It is not alive in the copy.** It “sleeps” and “wakes”, which the page
   says of apps already. It does not eat, feel, want or die. A lost one
   “goes out”.
7. **No rays on the sun itself.** Rays are the cartoon sun, and they are
   kept for one job: the faint dashed lines of glue between copies.

### It is not Carloku's block

| | The CARLOS sun | Carloku's block |
|---|---|---|
| Shape | A circle | A square block |
| Colour | Amber, with a glow | Lilac, flat |
| Face | None, ever | Two eyes; it blinks and dozes |
| Speaks by | Its light and its size | Its eyes |
| Stands for | A CARLOS app, from the inside | Your app with somewhere to live |

They meet once, at the end, as in round 3: the block with a magnifying
glass over it and the sun inside the glass.

### Drop-in: SVG

`sun.js` writes this. As a group inside a larger drawing:

```html
<g class="sun" data-state="rest" transform="translate(120 120)">
  <g class="sun-glow">
    <circle r="105"/><circle r="90"/><circle r="76.8"/><circle r="64.8"/>
  </g>
  <circle class="sun-pulse" r="60"/>
  <g class="sun-self">
    <circle class="sun-ring" r="60"/>
    <circle class="sun-body" r="36"/>
    <circle class="sun-core" r="13.8"/>
  </g>
</g>
```

As an element by itself, `sunSVG()` wraps the same group in
`<svg class="sun-svg" viewBox="-100 -100 200 200" aria-hidden="true">`
with the ring at 57, which leaves room for the glow. It is decoration: the
words beside it always say what it shows.

Classes on the group: `is-live` (it breathes and animates), `is-walled`
(heavy ring), `is-small` (no core, no glow), `on-floor` (it grows from its
foot). A parent with `sun-night` switches the colours for a dark ground.

`sun(cx, cy, r, o)` takes `state`, `thick`, `floor`, `live`, `op`,
`split` and `tilt` (the dividing pose), and `horizon` (a y below which it
is hidden, for setting). The quiz storyboard's older option names
(`sleep`, `wake`, `sad`, `ghost`) still work.

### Drop-in: CSS

`sun.css` is the whole of it, about sixty lines. The core:

```css
:root, .sun-day {
  --sun: #ffb454; --sun-hot: #ffc66e; --sun-pale: #f3c891;
  --sun-core: #fff0d2; --sun-edge: #c97a16; --sun-glow: .075;
}
.sun-night { --sun-edge: #ffb454; --sun-core: #fff3dc; --sun-glow: .05; }

.sun-glow circle { fill: var(--sun); fill-opacity: var(--sun-glow); }
.sun-ring { fill: none; stroke: var(--sun-edge); stroke-width: 1.5; opacity: .6; }
.sun-body { fill: var(--sun); stroke: var(--sun-edge); stroke-width: 1.5; }
.sun-core { fill: var(--sun-core); }
.sun-pulse { fill: none; stroke: var(--sun); stroke-width: 1.5; opacity: 0; }
.sun-self, .sun-glow, .sun-pulse { transform-box: fill-box; transform-origin: 50% 50%; }

.sun[data-state=swell]  .sun-self { transform: scale(1.18); }
.sun[data-state=squish] .sun-self { transform-origin: 50% 100%; transform: scale(1.2, .72); }
.sun[data-state=squish] .sun-body { fill: var(--sun-pale); }
.sun[data-state=squish] .sun-glow { opacity: .35; transform: scale(1.1, .8); }
.sun[data-state=asleep] .sun-self { transform: scale(.86); }
.sun[data-state=asleep] .sun-body { fill-opacity: .34; }
.sun[data-state=asleep] :is(.sun-core, .sun-glow) { opacity: 0; }
.sun[data-state=bright] .sun-self { transform: scale(1.08); }
.sun[data-state=bright] .sun-body { fill: var(--sun-hot); }
.sun[data-state=bright] .sun-glow circle { fill-opacity: calc(var(--sun-glow) * 1.7); }
.sun[data-state=bright] .sun-pulse { opacity: .5; transform: scale(1.3); }
.sun[data-state=ghost] :is(.sun-body, .sun-core, .sun-glow) { display: none; }
.sun[data-state=ghost] .sun-ring { stroke-dasharray: 4 5; opacity: .75; }

@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference) {
  .sun-self, .sun-glow { transition: transform .4s cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.3,1), opacity .4s ease; }
  .sun[data-state=asleep] :is(.sun-self, .sun-glow, .sun-body, .sun-core, .sun-ring) { transition-duration: 1.4s; }
  .sun[data-state=bright] :is(.sun-self, .sun-glow, .sun-body, .sun-core, .sun-ring) { transition-duration: .3s; }
  .sun.is-live[data-state=rest] .sun-self { animation: sun-breathe 4s ease-in-out infinite; }
  .sun.is-live[data-state=bright] .sun-pulse { animation: sun-pulse .6s ease-out 1 both; }
}
@keyframes sun-breathe { 50% { transform: scale(1.035); } }
@keyframes sun-pulse { from { opacity: .85; transform: scale(1); } to { opacity: .5; transform: scale(1.3); } }
```

Every state is a transform, an opacity or a fill. There is no script in
the character: a page changes `data-state` and the stylesheet does the
rest.

**Dividing** is the one move that needs two elements, and only the
homepage's chapter 2 uses it:

1. The sun swells (400 ms).
2. A copy of it appears on the same spot. The two slide apart along a
   slant of about 25 degrees, 0.62 r each way, over 500 ms with the
   deck's glide. Their bodies overlap as they part, which is all the
   “pinch” there is. The original ring stretches round both and fades.
3. Each draws its own ring (300 ms, `stroke-dashoffset`), and both settle
   to rest.

With reduced motion it is two stills: one sun, then two.

## What reads better as a sun, and what does not

Better:

- **Sleep is the sun's own language.** Dimming needs no explanation, and
  “set” is a picture a cell could not make: the idle copies sit half under
  their lines like a row of sunsets, and the one with a request has risen
  (frame `c3`). It also makes the cost point without a word: lights off
  when nobody is home.
- **Waking is brightening**, and it is quick. One ring goes out from the
  sun like a ripple, which also reads as “a request touched it”.
- **“Lose one” becomes a light going out** and being lit again from its
  copy (frame `c4`). “Lit again” is a better verb than “grows back”.
- **“No corners to cut”** is cleaner. A plain circle beside a square with
  its corners cut off is the whole joke; the wobbly cell muddied it
  (frame `u`).
- **The wall is a ring.** A request for next door's data stops at a clean
  line (frame `c2`). The membrane said the same thing less clearly.
- **The attributes hang on orbits.** In the hero the eleven words sit on
  two faint orbits round the sun (frame `h2`). The orbits do not turn.
- **Glue is faint rays.** Thin dashed lines run from each copy to the
  relay and the broker. Take the glue away and the rays go while every
  light stays on (frame `c6`).
- **“Nothing in the middle” gains a second reading.** A ring of suns
  round an empty centre is a solar system with no sun in the middle,
  because every copy is its own.
- **The rail is the character.** The six progress dots are the sun at its
  smallest, lit as each chapter is told.
- **The fizz belongs.** The small rings rising from “uncompromising” are
  now the same shape as the character.

Worse, or changed:

- **“Everything inside” loses its window.** The translucent cell showed
  its parts. The sun is opaque, so the four parts become four small points
  on an orbit inside its ring, each with a label (frame `c1`).
- **“It takes the shape of whatever holds it” is gone.** A circle does not
  pour into a box. Chapter 5 now shows the same circle, the same size, in
  four different holders (frame `c5`). That is arguably the truer picture:
  the point was always that nothing is rebuilt to fit.
- **A pair looks like eyes.** See rule 2.
- **It needs a dark edge on a light page.** The deck never had this
  problem.

One idea I did not draw: “globally distributed” as daylight, a horizon
with the sun up where the people using it are awake. It is the natural
picture for a sun, and it would overclaim. Round 3's caution stands: each
copy lives in one region at a time (question 4 there).

## Open questions for Paul

1. **Is this the sun you meant?** Body, core, ring and glow, as on the
   sheet. The deck's dot was a point with a halo; this is a little more
   solid so it holds on a light page.
2. **The core.** A paler circle at the centre, as you asked. It could also
   go: a plain amber disc in a ring is simpler and has no chance of
   reading as an eye. I kept it because it gives “inside” something to
   mean.
3. **The ring at rest.** Always there, as drawn, or only where the chapter
   is about the boundary? Always there makes it CARLOS's and not any
   orange dot.
4. **Amber beside the teal.** The page accent stays teal and the sun is
   the only amber thing, as in the deck. Or should “uncompromising” and
   the eyebrows turn amber too?
5. **Light by default.** The sun looks best on the dark theme (frame
   `h4`). Is the light page still the default?
6. **“Set” for asleep.** Half under a line, as in frame `c3`. Too cute, or
   right?
7. **The orbits in the hero.** Two faint ellipses behind the words. Keep,
   or leave the words loose?
8. **“At home anywhere”.** The same circle in every holder, in place of taking the
   holder's shape. Agreed?
9. **The cut corners.** Round 3's question 3, and a better joke now.
10. **Carloku.** The magnifying glass still, and may carloku.com use the
    picture back? (Round 3's question 9.)
11. **Still open from round 3:** “globally distributed” (4), the list
    (5), “private” (6), which draft (7), the cloud on a phone (8), and
