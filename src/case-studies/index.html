Case studies

Tito

Two years in three months

Using the CARLOS principles, Team Tito build their latest release that combined their 10 year old Rails app and their most recent Pro app. CARLOS allows building new features at a velocity not seen before, and allowing customers to locate their data in any one of 7 regions.

Jelly

New opportunities from on-prem installs

Jelly's ground-up rewrite using Rastrillo meant that it inherited performance budgets and clean separation of customer data, along with the ability to deploy on-prem, unlocking sales opportunities that were impossible before.
