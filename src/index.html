What makes CARLOS different?

By combining GenAI, old school computer science principles, and embracing modern tech, CARLOS rejects both monoliths and microservices, in favour of many small full-stack instances.

  • Single process apps

    By bundling everything into a single process, deployment and distribution becomes trivial.

  • Independent instances

    Each tenant, customer, team, or user gets an independent app instance, making data portability simple.

  • Deploy anywhere

    Since every tenant is self-contained, they can run on any host on any platform and still feel integrated.

A single-process CARLOS app provides its own self-contained web app, HTML, CSS, and JS. Each tenant can serve their own API, expose their own MCP, and provide a complete CLI: all in the same binary.

What about scaling? Across all tenants, you get planet-scale. Per-tenant, typical database writes per second on commodity CPUs are in the hundreds per second, and reads are in the thousands if not tens of thousands. Good problems to have when you get there.
