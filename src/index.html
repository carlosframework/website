What the letters buy you

Six simple promises.

  • Cost-efficientLightweight processes cost pennies to run, and sleep when idle.
  • AvailableMillisecond wake times with host failover.
  • ReplicatedEvery database streams to object storage from the first byte.
  • LightweightStatic, single-process binaries with embedded databases, system managed.
  • OpenOpen source, open infrastructure, and open data
  • SecureData isolated by default, with end-to-end encryption encouraged
