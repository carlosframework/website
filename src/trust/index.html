What can the host see?

ClassModelWhat stays readable
SStatelessNo stored user data.
A1Host-blindNetwork timing and object sizes. Content keys stay on users’ devices.
A2Host-blind, declared metadataOnly the metadata the app explicitly declares, alongside transport information.
A/pubPublic by designPublic content is readable. Private content stays encrypted.
BSealed at restThe running process can read data. Stored copies outside it remain encrypted.
CHost-sightedStored data is readable. Protection comes from tenant isolation and operator policies.

The A variants describe different models, not a ranking.

Declare it per workload

Record the class, readable surfaces and key locations for each workload. An encrypted app and its automation service can have different classes.

Your licence is a separate choice

Trust describes access to data. A software licence determines who may run, change and redistribute the code.

Make room for people and agents

Publish the tools and skills people can use to operate or adapt your app. Declare what is available today.
